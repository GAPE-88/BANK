How worthwhile is it to study IT?

GAPE Academy 6 min read IT · Computing

Technology now touches almost every part of modern life — banking, healthcare, commerce, transport, education. Behind all of it stand IT professionals. So how worthwhile is it really to study IT? Is it truly worth the time and money? Let's answer that honestly.

01. A field whose demand never fades

As the world accelerates its digital shift, demand for software, data and cybersecurity professionals keeps rising. The US Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) projects that jobs for software developers will grow much faster than the average for all occupations in the coming years.

Mongolia is no different. Local banks, fintechs, startups and international companies are constantly looking for programmers, systems analysts and data specialists. Yet IT students make up only a small share of all students — meaning demand outstrips supply, so jobs are waiting for well-prepared professionals.

02. High salaries and career growth

Because IT is a knowledge- and skill-based field, salaries tend to sit above the market average. Even a beginner who builds solid skills can raise their pay within a short time.

The IT career path is also remarkably flexible: you can choose from programming, web development, mobile app development, data analysis, cybersecurity, product management and more. Even after specializing in one area, moving into another remains wide open.

"IT isn't something you learn once and set aside — it's a path of lifelong growth that keeps pace with the world."

03. Work from anywhere in the world

The biggest advantage of an IT career is that it knows no borders. The ability to write code and design systems is valued the same everywhere. With an internationally recognized diploma or certification, you can:

  • Work remotely for foreign companies
  • Move abroad to study or work
  • Freelance and join international projects
  • Launch your own startup or product to the world

The key is having an internationally recognized educational foundation. This is exactly where the right choice of education matters.

04. Choosing the right program is half the battle

The future of IT is bright, but a "just learn to code" attitude isn't enough. Employers and universities value systematic, internationally standardized education. That is why a diploma and a degree still matter.

The NCC Education Diploma in Computing offered at GAPE Academy meets exactly this need:

  • Study at home — on-campus, project-based lessons
  • Continue abroad — transfer straight into Year 2/3 of a bachelor's at 55+ universities
  • Continue in Mongolia — earn a foreign university bachelor's online

This way you save money and time while following a real path to becoming an internationally valued IT professional.

In short

Studying IT is one of the smartest educational investments today because it is high in demand, well paid and workable from anywhere in the world. The key is choosing an internationally recognized, systematic program.
